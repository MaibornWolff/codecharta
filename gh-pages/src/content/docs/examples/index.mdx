# Examples

These open-source projects show CodeCharta on codebases from 30 thousand to 6 million lines of code. The large ones come from the [Apache Language Treemap](https://projects.apache.org/statistics.html).

Each page opens its maps in the [Web Studio](https://codecharta.com/visualization/app/index.html) with one click, and projects with two snapshots open straight in **Compare**. You can also download the `.cc.json` files. The maps were made between 2016 and 2019 in the cc.json 1.x format, which the Web Studio opens directly; to use them with `ccsh`, upgrade them with [`ccsh convert`](/docs/filter/convert) first.
